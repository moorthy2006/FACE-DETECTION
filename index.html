About FaceDetect

FaceDetect is a simple, browser-based tool for detecting human faces in a live camera feed or an uploaded photo. Every bit of processing happens locally on your device using face-api.js, a face detection library built on top of TensorFlow.js — nothing is uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. The Tiny Face Detector model loads once when you open the Face Detection page.
  2. Your camera frame or uploaded image is passed through the model.
  3. Detected faces are drawn as boxes, and the total count is shown instantly.

Good to know

  • Camera access requires your permission and a secure (HTTPS or localhost) connection.
  • Supported uploads: JPG, JPEG, and PNG.
  • Detection accuracy depends on lighting, angle, and image quality.
